Understand the structure of the material

These frameworks connect the key ideas behind the content units, glossary and action rules.

Problem map

Make boundaries, stakeholders, constraints and success criteria explicit.

Applies when: Entering an unfamiliar or poorly defined problem

Evidence chain

Connect conclusions to observations and competing explanations.

Applies when: Judging whether a claim is well supported

Action loop

Test an assumption, compare prediction and outcome, then revise.

Applies when: Turning understanding into a small informative action

Turn methods into practical actions

Total: 3 rules covering decisions and actions in the material. Each states a condition, action and reason.

  • When The problem scope is unclear → Draw a problem map first （The boundary helps identify relevant information）
  • When Confidence in a claim is low → Gather evidence and seek counterevidence （A single observation should not be treated as proof）
  • When Discussion stalls → Design a small informative test （Its result can update assumptions and guide the next action）
